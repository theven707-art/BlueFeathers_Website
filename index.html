About Blue Feathers Sports Club

Blue Feathers Sports Club is your ultimate destination for fitness, recreation, and wellness. Located in the heart of Moratuwa at Bandaranayake Mawatha, Kattubadda, we offer world-class facilities including a professional badminton court, modern gym, pristine swimming pool, and a delightful restaurant and bar. Whether you're looking to stay fit, play sports, learn swimming, or simply relax with friends and family, Blue Feathers has everything you need under one roof.
